## Conduct

-# The standards you're expected to follow for this Discord.

* Let people opt out gracefully.
* Share only content you have the rights/permission to share.
* Stay constructive by responding to ideas rather than making them personal.
* Disagreement is inevitable, but respect for others' views will earn you respect in return.
* Use <:SL:1367089201371414619>[Second Life maturity ratings](<https://community.secondlife.com/knowledgebase/english/maturity-ratings-r52/>) for designated <:SLRatingGeneral:1442512018853855332>**eneral**, <:SLRatingModerate:1442512044405559316>**oderate** and <:SLRatingAdult:1442512068916805715>**dult** areas.
* Treat all areas on this Discord as a <:SLRatingGeneral:1442512018853855332>**eneral** maturity rating unless specified otherwise.
* Celebrate creators by crediting them when sharing builds, scripts, or textures.
* Promotion is encouraged in our *Community Creations* section.
* Being in character is welcome!
