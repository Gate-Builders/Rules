## Conduct

* Disagreement is inevitable, but respect for others' views will earn you respect in return. Keep conversations kind and constructive by responding to ideas rather than making them personal.
* Use <:SL:1367089201371414619>[Second Life maturity ratings](<https://community.secondlife.com/knowledgebase/english/maturity-ratings-r52/>) for designated <:SLRatingGeneral:1442512018853855332> *General*, <:SLRatingModerate:1442512044405559316> *Moderate* and <:SLRatingAdult:1442512068916805715> *Adult* areas.
* Treat all areas on this Discord as a <:SLRatingGeneral:1442512018853855332> *General* maturity rating unless specified otherwise.
* Celebrate creators by crediting them when sharing builds, scripts, or textures.
* Promotion is encouraged in our *Community Creations* section.
* No unauthorised sharing of paywalled or copyrighted content.
* Being in character is welcome!
