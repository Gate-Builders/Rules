## Reporting rule violations

If something feels off, you're never *bothering* us by saying so.

### Discord

Tag or reach out to an <:Discord:1442537480917356624>`@Moderator`, or use our <:AF:1351928274795888711>[support ticket system](<https://www.alpha-fox.com/support/tickets/>).

### Stargates

* Check your Stargate ticket status via the <:AF:1351928274795888711>[Stargate Standards of Placement ticket system](<https://www.alpha-fox.com/support/ssop/>).
* To report a Stargate, click it in-world and select the reporting option, or visit the <:AF:1351928274795888711>[ASN database](<https://www.alpha-fox.com/asn/db/>) to find the Stargate's profile and report there.
